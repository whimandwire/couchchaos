    How to Play

    Pick or roll a mode, follow the prompts, and let chaos ensue. Each turn rolls the ingredients you need to make watching feel like a mini-challenge.

    • Mode-first (default) rolls only what that mode needs.
    • Content-first rolls a service and search prompt before the mode.
    • Use the dice tray to see exactly how timers were rolled.
    • Score keeping is optional—toggle it on in Settings.
    • Everything saves locally so you can jump back in anytime.
